Before You Start

Blue Top Training provides general strength, conditioning, and hitting development programming for informational purposes only. It is not medical advice, and it is not a substitute for evaluation by a physician, athletic trainer, or physical therapist.

Strength training, weighted bat work, and high-effort conditioning carry a real risk of injury, especially when technique, recovery, or training age aren't accounted for. Talk to a doctor before starting this or any training program, particularly if you have an existing injury, medical condition, or haven't trained consistently before.

Train within your ability. Stop any exercise that causes pain (soreness is normal; sharp or joint pain is not). Blue Top Training isn't liable for injuries resulting from use of this app.

By continuing, you confirm you understand these risks and are choosing to participate at your own discretion.
